What we collect

Only what you write when you send an order or ask for a special order:

  • your name and rank;
  • your phone / WhatsApp number and/or email;
  • your vessel, port and arrival day;
  • the products you order and your notes.

Your shopping list is saved only on your own phone or computer until you send it.

Why we use it

  • To prepare your order and deliver it on board.
  • To contact you about your order (stock, price, delivery time).
  • To send you offers and new products — only if you ticked that box. You can stop them at any time.

Who else sees it

  • Our order system runs on Google services (Google Sheets and Gmail).
  • To deliver on board, we may share your vessel name and delivery details with the ship's agent or the port terminal, only to arrange access and delivery.

We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice data: 6 years (Spanish commercial law).
  • Marketing consent: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to stop using it, or to stop sending you offers. Write to contact@theseamanshop.com.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency: www.aepd.es.
